Interpretation

0–3: Low risk. ≥4: High risk in the original Padua model.

The original cohort reported symptomatic VTE in 0.3% of low-risk patients and 11.0% of high-risk patients without prophylaxis over follow-up to 90 days. These are cohort observations, not individual predictions.

Risk assessment must be combined with bleeding assessment and clinical judgment.

Barbar et al., J Thromb Haemost 2010 · Original study ↗
